# Environment generation prompts

The built-in imagegen tool produced the three environment/device plates. The personal website scene was supplied by the project owner. These prompts describe the generated environment and hardware; App screens were added afterward by the deterministic compositor and were never generated or repainted.

Each scene used an initial environment prompt, followed by a hardware-specific edit using the official Apple reference linked in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). Generative results are not expected to reproduce byte for byte; the selected JPEG plates and their hashes are retained in [SCENES.json](SCENES.json). The compositor can reproduce the screen placement from those retained inputs.

## daylight-desk

### Environment

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: Wizard app lifestyle environment plate. Photorealistic editorial interior product photography, a believable classic black bezel iPad with a circular home button on the right in LANDSCAPE orientation, 4:3 display on a real physically plausible stand. The display is a perfectly flat featureless near-black rectangular insert, no UI, no letters or digits, no glow, no reflected window across it. ALL FOUR DISPLAY CORNERS are clearly visible, no objects overlap the display or device. Camera almost at display height with modest natural perspective, realistic classic tablet proportions and materials. Device takes roughly 55-65 percent of the image width, sits fully inside the frame. High-end interior magazine quality, tactile materials, gentle photographic grain, restrained color grading, authentic small imperfections. No people, no logos, no readable text on objects, no watermarks, no marketing lettering. Wide 3:2 composition, around 1536x1024. This is a background/device plate: real native App pixels will be inserted separately; do not invent a screen interface.  Scene: sunlit pale oak writing desk beside a tall window, sheer ivory linen curtain, diffuse late-morning daylight, one softly blurred green plant, a closed unlettered cream notebook and a handmade ivory ceramic cup toward the far side. Device is slightly left of center on a slim dark stand, screen plane almost front facing, empty surface in front. Palette: warm white, pale wood, natural olive green with subtle pastel peach. Quiet, spacious, lived-in, elegant without overstyling. Beautiful realistic shadow under the tablet, soft background blur. Do not include a keyboard.
```

### Hardware refinement

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Image1 is the target daylight desk photo. Image2 is Apple's official iPad2 product reference; use ONLY its physical white iPad2 hardware shape, not the iOS display. Change ONLY the tablet in Image1 into a photorealistic WHITE iPad2 (2011) in landscape orientation on the existing stand. Preserve the whole room, daylight, lens, objects and composition. iPad2 has notably broad even white bezels on all four sides, a gently curved silver aluminum back and 8.8mm rounded body; body face landscape ratio is 241.2:185.7 (about1.30), its 9.7-inch display is exact4:3 with SQUARE screen corners. Its active screen occupies about81.7percent of body width and79.6percent of body height. Old round white home button with pale square glyph on the RIGHT; a tiny camera on the LEFT. No Touch ID metal ring, no modern edge-to-edge screen. Make device proportions match the official reference, wider top/bottom bezels than the original target tablet. ALL FOUR active-display corners unobstructed. Display must be featureless near-black, no icons, clock, letters, numbers, logo or reflections. Keep beautiful material realism and gentle believable aging, no added text.
```
