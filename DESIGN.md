Artwork remains naturally colored. Gradients are framing and selected controls, not a blanket recolor of every photograph. Secondary text needs sufficient contrast against the actual surface.

## Native App — Operate / Experience
Full-bleed landscape media is the canvas. Use condensed heavy system display numerals and readable system/PingFang CJK controls. Do not bundle Rockstar fonts. Keep touch targets at least 44pt and preserve the existing menu model, editing, cancellation and Reduce Motion behavior. Use consistent plum-toned panels, quiet borders, cream labels and pink selection. Preserve persisted user transforms and stable identifiers.
